Project TItle: Enterprise Multimodal RAG Platform with Agentic Retrieval and Hybrid Search

Project Goal: Build an enterprise AI platform that allows u sers to upload organizational documents
              (PDFs, DOCX, PPTX, images, and scanned files) and interact with them through natural language. 
              The platform should provide accurate, cited answers using advanced retrieval techniques such as 
              hybrid search, reranking, and agentic workflows.

Functional Requirements:
    -Authentication
    -Login/Register
    -JWT authentication
    -User profiles

Document Upload
Supported formats:
    -PDF
    -DOCX
    -PPTX
    -PNG
    -JPG
    -Scanned PDFs
